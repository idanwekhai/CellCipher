03 Biological feasibility Planned · Mode 2

Not every sequence
can be made.

Digital mode optimises for clean round-tripping, not for the bench. On real text it produces long single-base runs and skewed GC content — both of which cause failures during chemical synthesis and sequencing. The stats ledger above reports them rather than hiding them.

A synthesis-safe mode has to encode under constraint. The reference approach is the yin–yang codec (YYC), which pairs two complementary rules to write two bits per nucleotide while keeping GC content and homopolymer runs inside what synthesis and sequencing tolerate — reporting 99.9% average recovery above 10⁴ molecule copies.

Ping, Z., Zhang, H., et al. “Towards practical and robust DNA-based data archiving using the yin–yang codec system.” Nature Computational Science 2, 234–242 (2022). s43588-022-00231-2 ↗
